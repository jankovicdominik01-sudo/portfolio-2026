Chapter I · Slovakia

Taste of Slovakia

Potatoes, sheep's cheese, cabbage and a lot of patience.

Kapustnica
Sauerkraut soup

With smoked sausage and mushrooms. The star of Christmas Eve.

Lokše
Potato pancakes

Thin and soft. Savoury with goose and red cabbage, or sweet with poppy seeds.

Štrúdľa
Strudel

Paper-thin dough rolled around apples, poppy seeds or sweet cheese.

Local tip: Skalický trdelník, a sweet pastry baked on a rolling spit, comes from Skalica, 20 km from Senica. It has EU-protected status.

Chapter II · Senica

Senica

more than a dot on the map

Why is Senica special?

  • Between two landscapes: the Little Carpathians and the pine forests of Záhorie.
  • History close by: castles, manor houses and a national shrine.
  • Everything is near: a lake 3 km away, Czechia 15 km, Austria and Vienna within reach.
